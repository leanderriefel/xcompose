# Privacy Policy for XCompose

Last updated: September 2, 2026

XCompose is a browser extension that adds user-initiated AI writing actions to the X/Twitter post and reply composer.

## User-facing disclosure

XCompose stores your settings and API keys locally. When you choose an action, your draft is sent over HTTPS to your selected OpenRouter or OpenCode Go provider to generate a rewrite. XCompose does not send data to its developer.

## Data XCompose stores

XCompose stores the following data locally in the browser using extension storage:

- Your selected AI provider and model
- API keys for OpenRouter and/or OpenCode Go
- Enabled actions and custom prompts, including general instructions

This data is used only to provide and remember the extension's settings. XCompose does not operate a server and does not transmit this stored data to the developer.

## Data sent to AI providers

When you explicitly choose an AI action, XCompose sends the draft text in the active X/Twitter composer and the API key for your selected provider to that provider over HTTPS. The provider returns the requested rewritten text.

XCompose supports OpenRouter and OpenCode Go. Their handling of data is governed by their respective privacy policies and terms. Do not submit information you do not want to share with your selected provider.

## Data XCompose does not collect

XCompose does not collect analytics, browsing history, usage telemetry, advertising identifiers, or data from pages other than X/Twitter. It does not sell, rent, or share data with the developer or advertisers.

## Your choices

You can change or remove API keys, prompts, and other settings in XCompose Settings. Uninstalling the extension or clearing its extension storage removes locally stored settings.
